# Scope of Work — Nigerian Tax Adviser

**Programme:** SuiteFlow hotel management platform — Nigerian pilot preparation
**Prepared:** 23 September 2026 · **Status:** engagement enquiry

## 1. Context

The programme is specifying and building a hotel management platform for a Nigerian pilot property (200 rooms, Lagos, NGN, West Africa Time). Before financial configuration is finalised, the programme requires a Nigerian tax adviser to confirm the tax treatment of hotel operations as implemented in the system. The current design deliberately labels all tax positions **unverified** pending your advice; the platform's tax rules are effective-dated configuration, not code, so your conclusions will be applied by configuration change with effect from the advice date.

## 2. Scope

Nigerian tax treatment of hotel operations in a property management system:

1. VAT tax point per revenue type (service delivery vs invoice vs payment vs receipt).
2. Confirmation of the tax-inclusive extraction method for cancellation/no-show penalties and deposit forfeitures (worked example supplied: ₦43,000 at 7.5% → ₦40,000 revenue + ₦3,000 tax, illustrative).
3. Whether the 10% service charge enters the VAT base, and the tax treatment of its distribution to staff.
4. Deposits, cancellations and no-show fees: taxable on receipt, application or forfeiture?
5. Current FIRS e-invoicing/fiscalisation obligations and timeline, applicability to hotels, and the appropriate integration approach (the platform treats accredited providers as adapters).
6. Required invoice particulars and any gapless-numbering/series rules for statutory documents.
7. Rounding and computation order rules.
8. Effective dates and any imminent changes.

## 3. Deliverables

- Written opinion covering the questions above.
- Per-tax-type tax-point values and an effective-dated mapping table.
- Worked examples matching the platform's golden-day fixture: room night; F&B with service charge; deposit forfeiture; no-show with and without deposit.
- A sign-off statement suitable for the programme's deployment gate.
- Open-items memo (positions that remain uncertain and how to handle them).

## 4. Inputs provided by the programme

- Financial architecture summary and the tax questions above.
- Illustrative worked examples and the golden-day scenario outline.
- Access to programme staff for clarification.
