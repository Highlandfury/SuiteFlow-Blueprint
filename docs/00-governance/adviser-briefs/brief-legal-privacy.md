# Scope of Work — Legal / Privacy Counsel

**Programme:** SuiteFlow hotel management platform — Nigerian pilot preparation
**Prepared:** 23 September 2026 · **Status:** engagement enquiry

## 1. Context

The programme is specifying and building a hotel management platform for a Nigerian pilot property (200 rooms, Lagos). It processes guest and employee personal data, including identity documents. Before any real personal data is loaded, the programme requires legal counsel to confirm its privacy and data-protection position under the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023** and related regulation (including the NDPC's General Application and Implementation Directive). All legal positions in the current design are labelled **unverified** pending your advice.

## 2. Scope

1. Confirmation or amendment of the retention schedule: guest ID images (proposed 90 days), guest profiles (6 years), financial records (7 years), AML-relevant records (5 years), audit/security logs (12 months+), CCTV (30 days).
2. Data-subject access request (DSAR) workflow, statutory timelines and evidence requirements.
3. Breach notification duties and clocks (NDPC reporting, data-subject notification), to confirm/refine the programme's incident decision tree.
4. Lawful basis mapping per processing activity (reservations, registration, payments, marketing consent, employee data).
5. Cross-border transfer conditions for hosting, support providers and messaging vendors (processor register requirements).
6. Whether identity document scanning is legally required at Nigerian hotel registration (and any foreign-guest reporting obligations).
7. Treatment of unclaimed guest deposits — escheatment, retention or continued liability.
8. Employee-data and consent requirements relevant to the platform.

## 3. Deliverables

- Legal memo covering the questions above.
- Confirmed retention schedule.
- Breach-notification matrix (what, to whom, when).
- Processor/transfer register requirements and DPAs checklist.
- DSAR design confirmation (intake, verification, clocks, refusal grounds).
- Statements that can be cited in the programme's design documents.

## 4. Inputs provided by the programme

- Security and privacy model summary; data-classification scheme; retention direction.
- Incident/breach procedure draft and the interface inventory touching personal data.
