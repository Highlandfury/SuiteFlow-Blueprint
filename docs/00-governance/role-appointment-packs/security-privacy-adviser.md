# Appointment Brief — Security / Privacy Adviser (part-time governance role)

**SuiteFlow hotel platform programme · September 2026**

## The programme

SuiteFlow is a hotel management platform being built for a Nigerian pilot (200-room Lagos property profile). The target-state blueprint is complete and entering acceptance; delivery runs to a pilot-ready reference release on **10 March 2027**. The programme follows a documented governance charter with explicit approval authorities. The pilot is currently a **synthetic reference environment** (no real guest or financial data), and privacy positions are deliberately marked unverified until Nigerian counsel/adviser confirmation.

**Who this suits:** an NDPC-licensed Data Protection Compliance Organisation (DPCO) or an experienced security/privacy practitioner. Using a licensed DPCO gives the programme clear regulatory grounding.

## The role

**Purpose:** own security and privacy sign-off and incident oversight — the security authority named in the programme charter.

**What you approve (scoped list):**
- The security model and role/authority matrix, including authorization enforcement (ADR-009…011 security boundaries).
- The incident and breach response procedure, and privacy operations (retention, DSAR workflow, processor/transfer gate).
- The AI enablement gate (no class-A/B capability without adviser sign-off and a DPIA).
- Export controls and the UAT role-account regime.
- The security test pack; participation in the reference-release tabletop exercise.

**What the role does not do:** operate the platform or perform transactions; grant itself access; approve spend or personnel.

## Time commitment (provisional)

- Orientation and initial review: ~2–3 hours (an AI role-assistant has already prepared review conclusions and sign-off packs).
- Ongoing: **2–4 hours/month**, plus escalation availability for security-incident declaration during the pilot (business-hours reachable; on-call may contain first).
- Acceptance sitting: one 90-minute session (or by 14 October for the first sitting).
- All meetings can be remote.

## Independence and term

- Independence from platform administration and from any vendor delivering the platform; conflicts declared and recorded.
- Term to the First-Property Deployment Gate review (renewable). Appointment recorded in the programme register; advisory-only interim arrangements end on your appointment.

## Commercial

Fee/retainer by agreement (part-time governance structure, quarterly in arrears); reasonable expenses; mutual NDA as needed.
