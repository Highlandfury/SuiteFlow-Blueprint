# Appointment Brief — Technical Lead (part-time governance role)

**SuiteFlow hotel platform programme · September 2026**

## The programme

SuiteFlow is a hotel management platform being built for a Nigerian pilot (200-room Lagos property profile). The target-state blueprint is complete and entering acceptance; delivery runs to a pilot-ready reference release on **10 March 2027**. The programme follows a documented governance charter with explicit approval authorities, and an independent acceptance review has already resolved all findings. The pilot is currently a **synthetic reference environment** (no real property or guest data).

## The role

**Purpose:** own the programme's technical integrity and technical sign-offs — the architecture authority named in the programme charter.

**What you approve (scoped list):**
- Target architecture, domain model, data model and state-machine designs.
- Architecture decisions ADR-001…004 and ADR-010/011 (topology/ownership, tenancy, temporal data, identity/numbering, outbox/idempotency, payments adapter); security boundaries with the Security/Privacy Adviser.
- Non-functional requirements, deployment and recovery architecture, and the QA strategy.
- Phase sequencing corrections and the technical release gate.
- Technical acceptance at the reference release.

**What the role does not do:** change business requirements, financial policy or prices; run the platform day to day; approve spend or personnel.

## Time commitment (provisional)

- Orientation and initial review: ~3 hours (an AI role-assistant has already prepared review conclusions and sign-off packs, so your time goes to decisions).
- Ongoing: **3–5 hours/month** (design reviews, a monthly governance check-in, query responses).
- Acceptance sitting: one 90-minute session (or by 14 October for the first sitting).
- All meetings can be remote.

## Independence and term

- Independence from any vendor delivering the platform; conflicts declared and recorded.
- Term to the First-Property Deployment Gate review (renewable). Appointment recorded in the programme register; interim arrangements (maintaining architect as interim design direction) end on your appointment.

## Commercial

Fee/retainer by agreement (part-time governance structure, quarterly in arrears); reasonable expenses; mutual NDA as needed.
